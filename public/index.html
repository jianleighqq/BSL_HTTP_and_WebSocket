About

I'm a third-year BS Information Technology student at MSU-IIT, and I like the part of computing most people never see: the switches, cables and rules that decide how machines talk to each other.

Most of my time goes into network design, software-defined networking and figuring out how to contain a threat quickly once something looks wrong. I care about systems that keep working when things go sideways.

Projects

01

NetworkGuard-SME

A threat containment framework built around small and mid-sized networks. Wazuh and Suricata watch for suspicious activity, and a Python layer in the middle reacts by cutting off the affected traffic through dynamic microsegmentation.

Built with Wazuh SIEM, Suricata, Python

02

Enterprise Switched and Routed Network

A multi-switch campus topology I designed and tested in GNS3. It uses Rapid-PVST+ to keep the layer 2 loop-free, LACP EtherChannel for link aggregation, inter-VLAN routing, and dual-stack IPv4 and IPv6 addressing with DHCPv6.

Built with GNS3, Cisco IOS, EtherChannel, DHCPv6
